## What
- Reduce the remaining Machines host bridge so `MainWindow` no longer directly implements a Machines bridge interface.
- Introduce `MachinesWorkspaceShellBridge` as a narrow shell-owned adapter consumed by `MachinesWorkspaceComposition`.
- Keep `MachinesWorkspaceComposition` as the effective host boundary for controller coordination while preserving the existing Machines workflow.
- Update AM structural tests to assert that the shell now provides helper callbacks through the bridge object instead of acting as the bridge type.

## Why
- AM35 introduced the correct Machines-local composition owner, but `MainWindow` still served as the concrete shell bridge type.
- That left capability-local controller coordination partially terminating in the shell.
- This slice reduces that boundary without redesigning Machines behavior or doing the final MainWindow cleanup.
