## What
- Introduce `MachinesWorkspaceComposition` as the long-lived Machines-local composition owner.
- Move Machines view, workspace viewmodel, controller wiring, and Machines-local UI coordination entrypoints behind that composition object.
- Reduce `MainWindow` to shell hosting plus a narrow Machines shell bridge for route activity, readiness polling, and delete dialogs.
- Update AM structural tests to assert the new composition-owner boundary and preserved long-lived workspace model.

## Why
- AM33/AM34 explicitly called out that `MainWindow` was still the practical Machines composition hub.
- Machines already had extracted state and orchestration seams, but the view/controller/viewmodel relationship still terminated in the shell.
- This slice introduces the composition owner only, so later issues can reduce the remaining host bridge without redesigning Machines behavior.
